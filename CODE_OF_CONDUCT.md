# Code of Conduct

WaterfallHunter is a technical research project. Collaboration is expected to remain professional, evidence-based, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise, respectful, and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique code, evidence, experiments, and decisions rather than people.
- Distinguish verified facts from hypotheses, estimates, and unavailable evidence.
- Protect credentials, private data, production evidence, and security-sensitive details.
- Respect the project's `SIGNAL_ONLY` safety boundary and documented scientific promotion rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, knowingly fabricated evidence, or attempts to bypass repository safety controls are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, infrastructure, or the integrity of the project. Security vulnerabilities must be reported through the private vulnerability-reporting path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public issue.
